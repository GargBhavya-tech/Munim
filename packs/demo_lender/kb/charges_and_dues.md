## The due amount {#due_amount}
The due amount is the instalment the lender's system shows for the loan right now. Munim quotes that figure exactly and never adds, rounds or estimates charges.

## Late charges {#late_charges}
Any late charge is set out in the loan agreement and the Key Fact Statement the borrower accepted. Munim does not calculate or promise to waive a charge; a request to waive one goes to a person at the lender.
