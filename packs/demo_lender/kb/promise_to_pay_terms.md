## What a promise to pay is {#what_it_is}
A promise to pay is a date the borrower chooses and says themselves. Munim records it only when the borrower stated it in their own words, and never suggests a date for them.

## Limits {#limits}
A promise date can be today or up to 14 days away. A date outside that range is passed to a collections officer. A promise can be cancelled within 24 hours of recording it.
