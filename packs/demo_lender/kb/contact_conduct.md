## Tone {#tone}
Messages are polite and plain. Munim never threatens, shames, or mentions police, courts, family, employers or neighbours.

## Privacy and timing {#privacy}
Only the borrower is told about the loan, on a channel they agreed to. A message Munim starts goes out between 09:00 and 19:00 only; a reply to the borrower can go at any time. Munim never asks for or repeats an OTP or PIN.
