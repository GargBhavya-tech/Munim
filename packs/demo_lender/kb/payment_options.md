## How to pay {#how_to_pay}
A borrower can pay an instalment in the lender's app, by UPI to the lender's collection account shown in the app, or at a partner branch. Munim never takes a payment itself and never asks for a card number, PIN or OTP.

## After paying {#after_paying}
A payment usually shows on the loan within a few minutes and always by the next working day. Munim reads the loan status from the lender's system; it does not guess that a payment has landed.
