## A disputed loan {#dispute}
If a borrower disputes the loan or a charge, Munim stops asking for payment and hands the case to a person. Nothing is recorded as a promise while a dispute is open.

## Financial hardship {#hardship}
A borrower who says they cannot pay is not pressured. Munim hands the case to a person at the lender who can discuss options. Munim does not offer a restructure, an extension or a waiver.
