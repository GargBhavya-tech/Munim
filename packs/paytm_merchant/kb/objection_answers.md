## Charges feel too high {#charges_too_high}
Explain the charge in rupees per payment rather than in percentages, and show what the merchant gets: the Soundbox announces every payment, settlements arrive in the bank account, and support is available in their language. Do not promise a discount; offer a waiver only within the band.

## Already using another QR {#already_using_other}
Many shops use more than one QR. The merchant can keep the other QR and add Paytm so that customers who prefer Paytm can pay. There is no obligation to remove the other QR.

## Device rent {#device_rent}
The Soundbox plan covers the device, its upkeep and replacement for device faults. The first month fee can be waived only within the approved band.

## Not now {#not_now}
Accept the answer politely. Ask when it would be good to call back, record the preference, and do not contact again before then. A clear no ends the conversation.

## I do not trust it {#trust}
Say that Paytm never asks for a PIN or OTP, that offers can be seen inside the Paytm for Business app, and that the merchant can ask for a person at any time. Do not send payment or download links to a new contact.
