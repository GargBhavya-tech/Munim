## Fake payment screens {#fake_screens}
A customer may show a screen that says paid even though no money arrived. The screen on a customer's phone is not proof. Only your own Paytm transactions or the Soundbox announcement show a real payment.

## How Munim checks {#how_munim_checks}
Munim reads the amount and time from the screenshot and then looks up the merchant's own transactions in a ten minute window. If no matching payment is found it says so and asks the merchant not to hand over goods yet.

## If it is unclear {#unclear}
If the picture is unclear or several payments could match, Munim asks the merchant for the amount and time instead of guessing.

## Reporting {#reporting}
Repeated fake screens are recorded and can be reported to the safety team.
