## First checks {#first_checks}
Check that the Soundbox is switched on and the battery is charged. Check that the charging cable and adapter are firmly connected. Check the signal: move the device away from metal shelves and thick walls. Ask the merchant to say what the lights show.

## Remote steps {#remote_steps}
Munim can read the device status, restart it remotely, switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, and send a one rupee test payment to check that it announces. These steps run in a fixed order and each result is checked against the device status, not against what the merchant says.

## Confirming it works {#confirm_working}
A fix is confirmed only when the device shows ONLINE and the test payment is announced out loud. Saying that it is working is not enough on its own.

## When a technician is needed {#technician}
A technician visit is raised when the fault looks like hardware damage, when the remote steps are used up, when the merchant cannot do the steps, when the merchant asks for a visit, or when the same device has failed before.

## Area outages {#area_outage}
When many devices in the same area lose connection together the cause is usually the network, not each device. Munim tells the merchant that the network is down and the device will reconnect by itself, and does not order replacements.
