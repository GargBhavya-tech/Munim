## Billing date {#billing_date}
The monthly plan fee is charged on the billing date shown in the app. A plan change made before the billing date takes effect on that date.

## Disputed charge {#disputed_charge}
If a merchant says a charge is wrong Munim looks at the plan, the billing date and any waiver. A waiver within the band can be applied. A bigger adjustment is sent to the sales lead.

## Renewal {#renewal}
Before renewal Munim tells the merchant the plan and the monthly fee from the system. The merchant can keep the plan, change it or decline.
