## Charging {#charging}
Charge the Soundbox with its own adapter. A full charge lasts a working day. If the battery is low the device speaks a warning.

## Volume and language {#volume_language}
Use the buttons on the side to change the volume. The language of announcements can be changed in the Paytm for Business app, and Munim can change the language preference on request.

## The Paytm button {#paytm_button}
The big button talks to the AI assistant. Press it, speak, and release. The assistant answers in the language the merchant prefers.

## Moving the device {#moving}
Keep the Soundbox away from water and heat and where the mobile signal is strong.
