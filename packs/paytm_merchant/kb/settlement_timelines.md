## Normal timing {#normal_timing}
Payments received on one day are settled to the bank account by 10 am on the next day. The system shows the expected time for each settlement and Munim quotes only that time.

## Pending or delayed {#delayed}
A settlement can be delayed by a bank holiday or a slow bank. Munim checks the settlement status, tells the merchant the expected time from the system, and follows up at that time.

## Held for review {#held_review}
Some settlements are held for a safety review. These always go to a risk reviewer, who decides. Munim says that the settlement is being reviewed and that it is sending the case to the risk team, and does not give a date unless the system returns one.

## Bank failure {#bank_failure}
If the bank rejects the credit the merchant must fix the bank details using the secure link in the app. Once the bank is verified the settlement is retried.
