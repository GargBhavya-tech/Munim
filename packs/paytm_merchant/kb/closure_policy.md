## Closing the account {#closing}
Munim makes one offer to fix the problem if the closure is because of a fault. If the merchant repeats that they want to close, the request is processed and not delayed.

## Cancelling a closure {#cancel_window}
A closure request can be cancelled for 48 hours after it is created. After that a person must help.

## Returning the device {#device_return}
A pickup is scheduled for the Soundbox. The merchant keeps it switched off and packed until the pickup. Pending settlements are still paid to the bank account.

## After closure {#after_closure}
No further messages are sent about offers after a closure. Records the law requires are kept; other merchant memory is deleted on request.
