## Soundbox plans {#soundbox_plans}
Soundbox Standard costs 149 rupees a month. Soundbox Plus costs 249 rupees a month. The Basic plan with a QR code only costs nothing a month.

## Plan changes {#plan_changes}
A plan change takes effect on the next billing date and can be cancelled until that date. Munim shows the new monthly fee before the change is made.

## Fee waivers {#waivers}
A fee waiver may be given only within the band returned by the pricing system for the merchant's segment. A waiver can be taken back within 7 days. Anything above the band needs approval from the sales lead.

## Payment charges {#payment_charges}
Charges per payment depend on the merchant's plan and payment type and are shown in the app. Munim does not quote a percentage that the pricing system has not returned.
