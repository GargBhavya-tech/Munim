## Accepted documents {#accepted_docs}
A shop licence, GST certificate or Udyam certificate is accepted as business proof. For identity, the owner's PAN and an address proof are used. The merchant uploads documents in the Paytm for Business app. Documents are never accepted in chat, by message or by voice.

## Why a KYC can be rejected {#rejection_reasons}
The most common reason is DOC_BLURRY: the photo is unclear, cut off or has glare. Other reasons are NAME_MISMATCH, where the name on the document differs from the name on the account, and DOC_EXPIRED. A rejected KYC can be submitted again; the app shows the reason.

## How to resubmit {#resubmit}
Munim can send a secure resume link that opens the KYC step where the merchant stopped. The link expires after 48 hours. Tips for a clear photo: place the document on a flat surface, use daylight, keep all four corners in the frame, and avoid flash.

## What Munim cannot do {#limits}
Munim cannot approve a KYC, cannot change KYC details and cannot look at documents. Approval is decided by the KYC team after the merchant submits. If the merchant says the KYC is done, Munim checks the system status before saying anything.
