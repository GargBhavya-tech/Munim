## What a risk hold is {#what_it_is}
A risk hold pauses a settlement while the risk team checks it. It does not mean the merchant has done anything wrong.

## Who decides {#who_decides}
Only a risk reviewer can release or keep a hold. Munim and support executives cannot clear it.

## What Munim says {#what_munim_says}
Munim tells the merchant that the settlement is under review, that the case has been sent to the risk team, and that a person will call with the outcome. It does not guess when the money will arrive.
