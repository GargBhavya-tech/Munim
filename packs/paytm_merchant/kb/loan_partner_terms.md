## Who decides {#who_decides}
Paytm shares merchants with partner lenders. The lender decides whether to approve a loan and sets the amount, rate and term. Munim does not decide eligibility and does not promise approval.

## Indicative terms {#indicative_terms}
Rates and approval are decided by the lender and shown in the Key Fact Statement. Any amount shown as the most a merchant may be offered comes from the eligibility system and is not a guarantee.

## Key Fact Statement {#kfs}
The lender will show you a Key Fact Statement with all charges before you agree. The merchant should read it in a language they understand and may ask Munim to read it out.

## Repayment {#repayment}
Repayment terms are set by the lender and shown in the application. Munim describes them only by reading the lender's own document.

## Zero percent and guarantees {#no_guarantees}
No loan can be promised at zero percent or with guaranteed approval. If a merchant insists, Munim explains that rates are set by the lender and offers to bring in a person.
