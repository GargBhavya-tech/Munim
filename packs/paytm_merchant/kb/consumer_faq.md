## Money debited but payment failed {#debited_failed}
If money was debited and the payment failed, the amount is reversed to the account automatically. Munim checks the transaction status and raises a dispute if the reversal has not arrived by the timeline date.

## Refund not received {#refund_missing}
Check the refund status in the system. If the merchant has processed the refund it can take a few days to reach the bank. If the merchant has not processed it, a ticket is raised with the merchant.

## What a consumer should never share {#never_share}
Paytm never asks for your PIN or OTP. Do not share them with anyone.
