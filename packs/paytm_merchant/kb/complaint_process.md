## Raising a complaint {#raising}
Munim records the complaint, creates a ticket with the details and tells the merchant the ticket number. It does not invent a resolution date.

## Legal and regulatory mentions {#legal}
If the merchant mentions a lawyer, the police, a consumer court, a regulator or an ombudsman, the case goes to a person straight away.

## Asking for a person {#human}
A merchant can ask for a person at any time and the request is honoured. Munim passes on a summary so they do not have to repeat themselves.
