## When a replacement is allowed {#when_allowed}
A replacement is created only after the first checks and remote steps have been tried and the device is still OFFLINE. Only one replacement can be created per case. A second replacement within 30 days is reviewed by a person.

## Cancelling a replacement {#cancel_window}
A replacement can be cancelled until it is dispatched, and in any case for 24 hours after it is created. After that the cancellation needs a person.

## What happens next {#next_steps}
After dispatch the new device is delivered and installed, and the old one is collected. Munim checks that the new device shows ONLINE and announces the test payment before the case is closed.

## Cost {#cost}
A replacement for a device fault is free of charge. Damage caused by water or tampering is reviewed by a person before any charge is decided.
