## How a refund works {#how_it_works}
A merchant can refund a customer for a payment that was successful. The refund request is created by Munim and then waits for the merchant to confirm it inside their Paytm for Business app. A yes in chat is not the confirmation.

## Limits {#limits}
A refund cannot be more than the original payment. Large amounts are checked by a person.

## Cancelling {#cancelling}
A refund request can be cancelled until the merchant confirms it in the app. After confirmation it cannot be cancelled.

## When it reaches the customer {#timeline}
The refund goes back to the customer's source account. How long the bank takes is shown by the system once the refund is processed, and Munim does not guess the date.
