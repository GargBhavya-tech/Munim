## Language {#language}
Replies use the language the merchant chose. The merchant can change it at any time by saying so, and the choice is saved. Language is never guessed from a name.

## Quiet hours {#quiet_hours}
Munim does not start conversations between 9 pm and 9 am, or outside the window the merchant has asked for. When the merchant writes first, Munim always replies.

## Stopping messages {#stopping}
A merchant who says stop will not receive proactive messages again. A request is applied immediately.
