## Money debited but payment failed {#debited_failed}
Failed payments are reversed automatically within the payment network's timeline, and Munim tracks the case. The exact number of days is not stated here until the official circular has been read.

## What Munim does {#what_munim_does}
Munim checks the transaction status, raises a dispute ticket with the transaction reference, and schedules a follow-up at the timeline date returned by the system. It never promises a date that the system has not returned.

## If the timeline is missed {#timeline_missed}
If the reversal has not arrived by the stated date the case goes to a person immediately. Compensation rules are applied by the payments team, not decided by Munim.

## Customer says they paid {#customer_paid}
A customer's screen is not proof of payment. Check the merchant's own transactions, or the Soundbox announcement. Do not hand over goods until the payment shows as received.
